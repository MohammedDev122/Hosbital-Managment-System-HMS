# Hosbital-Managment-System-HMS
Hospital Managment System It stimulate the patient's journey from booking a diagnosis to geting prescription or operation
##HMS Notes
## 1. Login and Access Control

When you open the application, you are met with a Login Screen. Users must enter their Login Name and Password to access the system.

    Role-Based Access: Once logged in, the features and functions available depend on the user's Department and Permission Level.

    Authority Levels: Each department has two levels of authority:

        Head of Department: A single individual with full administrative control over their section.

        Staff Members: The rest of the department personnel with standard operational permissions.

The system automatically identifies the user’s department and authority level upon sign-in.
## 2. Nursing Department (Head Nurse / Nurse)

If you log in as a Head Nurse, the system displays several options:

    Your Reservations: A schedule showing dates and service statuses. This helps prevent scheduling conflicts or overlaps when adding new services.

    My Operations: A list of operations where you are a member. You can search or filter through these. Members can update certain data (dates, crew members, or operation status), but cannot update the Patient ID, the Medical Record, or add new operations (as only doctors can initiate them).

    Diagnosis: A screen containing lists of booked, canceled, and finished diagnoses.

        Adding Diagnosis: Nurses can right-click to "Add" a new diagnosis. You must provide the Patient ID and Doctor ID (via selection lists) and the date/time.

        Validation: The system warns you of major errors (e.g., choosing a doctor with an incompatible specialization). Minor errors (like forgetting to set the status) are auto-corrected.

        Billing: Upon success, a Bill ID is generated.

        Restrictions: Nurses can edit dates or cancel a diagnosis, but they cannot change the status to "Finished." The system will warn: "You are not the Doctor."

    Patient Logs: Allows viewing all patient diagnoses or operations using filters like Service ID, Patient ID, or Name.

    Patient Management: A list of all patients in the hospital database. Receptionists/Nurses can add or edit patient info. However, Bank Account info requires a password for any modifications.

    Doctor Reservations: A view-only list to help find free time slots for booking diagnoses.

    Manage Nurse (Head of Department only):

        Nurse Option: Edit nurse info or fire a staff member. Once fired, their "due date" becomes the date of their last service, and they cannot be assigned to new tasks.

        Nurse Reservations: View the schedules of all nurses in the department.

    My Logs & My Info: View login history and edit personal profile information.

## 3. Accounting Department (Head Accountant / Accountant)

If logged in as the Head Accountant, the system provides:

    Payments: A list of paid, canceled, or pending bills.

        Editing Bills: Accountants cannot create bills (bills are generated by services). When editing a pending bill, the accountant's ID is temporarily attached. Once the transaction is finalized (Paid or Canceled), the ID becomes permanent. If it remains pending, the ID is removed.

        Restrictions: Finished or canceled bills cannot be re-edited. The service cost is determined by the Head of the Doctor’s Department and cannot be changed by the accountant.

        Payment Methods: Supports Cash or Visa. Visa transactions require an Account ID and Password. Errors include "Wrong ID/Password" or "Insufficient Money."

    Manage Accountants (Head of Department only): Ability to add, edit, or fire accountants.

## 4. Medical Department (Head Doctor / Doctor)

The Doctor's dashboard includes:

    My Diagnosis: Doctors see only their assigned diagnoses.

        Finishing a Diagnosis: To set a diagnosis to "Finished," the bill must be Paid.

        Scenarios: If the patient is fine, the doctor finishes the diagnosis. If the patient is ill, the doctor writes notes and issues a Medical Record, which is required for prescriptions or operations.

        Cancellation: Canceling a diagnosis automatically cancels the associated bill.

    My Operations: Doctors can add operations based on their issued Medical Records. They can select the operating room and add staff members (Doctors and Nurses).

        Staffing: If a doctor does not add themselves to the operation, they lose the ability to edit it later.

        Conflict Check: Changing an operation's time triggers a system check for staff overlaps; members with conflicts are automatically removed.

    Medical Records & Prescriptions: Doctors manage records and issue prescriptions. Medications can be added to a prescription as long as it is not canceled or suspended.

    Manage Doctor (Head of Department only):

        Can view/cancel all diagnoses but cannot "Finish" them (only the assigned doctor can).

        Manage doctor lists, specializations, and the Service Main list (to set service costs).

## 5. Pharmacy Department (Head Pharmacist / Pharmacist)

    Pharmacy Record: Pharmacists create records based on a Prescription ID. The system automatically pulls patient info and records the Pharmacist's ID.

    Cart & Payment: Prescribed medicines are added to a cart; they can only be collected after the bill is paid.

    Manage Pharmacist (Head of Department only): Manage pharmacists, medications, and medical categories.
